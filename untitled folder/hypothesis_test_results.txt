HYPOTHESIS TEST RESULTS

Dataset size after cleaning and merging: 671 movies

H1: There is a relationship between vote count and IMDb score.
Pearson correlation = 0.521
p-value = 5.69927e-48

H2: Average IMDb scores are different across genres.
Genres used in ANOVA: Action & Adventure, Dramas, Comedies, Children & Family Movies, Horror Movies, Classic Movies
ANOVA F-statistic = 30.613
p-value = 6.71522e-28

H3: Movies released before 2000 and after 2000 have different average IMDb scores.
Mean IMDb score (before 2000) = 6.866
Mean IMDb score (2000 and after) = 6.483
Welch t-statistic = 3.684
p-value = 0.00029439
